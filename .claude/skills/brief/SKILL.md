# /brief — Daily CEO Brief

1. Read `company/memory/state.md`, `company/strategy/90-day-plan.md` (current week), and
   `company/memory/decisions.md` (last 5 entries).
2. If the Owner's calendar or inbox connectors are available, pull today's events and anything
   from prospects or customers. Otherwise skip, do not ask.
3. Produce the brief in this exact shape, under 200 words:

```
MEDINA DIGITAL AI — DAILY BRIEF — {date}
Phase: {phase}   Week {n} of 90-day plan   Priority this week: {one line}

Scoreboard: revenue ${x}/mo · customers {n} · conversations this week {n}

Today, the team does:
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

Today, only YOU can do:
- [ ] ... (why it matters, deadline)
- [ ] ...

One thing to think about: {a sharp question or push}
```

4. Dispatch the day's agent work (Scout, Closer, Marketer, Builder) in parallel if the Owner
   says go, or if standing delegation exists in `decisions.md`.
5. Have the Chief of Staff update `state.md` with today's date and commit.

Tone: New York energy. Direct. Push if the Owner's to-dos from yesterday are still open.
