# Lessons

What we learned, so we do not pay for it twice. Newest first. Reviewed in `/weekly`.

## 2026-10-21 — Founding
- The research is clear: the first million is earned and saved, then invested. Nobody trades their
  way there from zero. Structure the business around cash flow.
- An AI team is leverage, not a business. The business is a customer paying for an outcome.
- Contribution rate beats return rate for the first million. $5k/month saved beats 10% returns.
- Every survivor in the AI-services research was specialized, productized and recurring. Every
  failure was generic, custom and one-off.
- Memory written in cloud sessions lands on a `claude/` branch. It needs a merge to reach the Mac.
