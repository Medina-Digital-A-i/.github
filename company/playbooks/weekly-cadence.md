# Playbook: Weekly Cadence

| Day | What | Who | Output |
|-----|------|-----|--------|
| Mon | `/brief` + dispatch week's work | CEO, all agents | Priorities set, agents running |
| Tue-Thu | Execution. Outreach, delivery, content, builds | Closer, Builder, Marketer | Drafts for Owner, shipped work |
| Fri | Pipeline and numbers check | Closer, CFO | Updated pipeline, cash view |
| Sat | `/upgrade` scan | Scout | Upgrade report, stack updates |
| Sun | `/weekly` review and next week's plan | CEO, Chief of Staff, CFO | Weekly review, state updated, committed |

Monthly (first Sunday): CFO finance review. Savings rate, investments, tax set-aside, wealth OS
milestones. Quarterly: thesis review by Strategist. Kill, double down, or pivot.
