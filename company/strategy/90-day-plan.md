# 90-Day Plan — Zero to First Clients

_Clock starts the day the Owner approves the thesis. Owner effort: 5-6 hours a day on the heavy
days, 2 hours minimum. Targets are the median case; the structure can hold the optimistic case._

## Targets

| Day | Median target | Optimistic |
|-----|---------------|------------|
| 30 | 1 paying client (pilot or $1.5-2.5k/mo) | 2 clients |
| 60 | 2-3 clients, $3-6k MRR | 3-4 clients, $5-8k MRR |
| 90 | 3-4 clients, $5-8k MRR, first case study | 5-7 clients, $8-12k MRR |

## Days 1-10: Pick the vertical, build the 15-minute demo

**Owner**
- [ ] Approve thesis. Fill in `company/memory/boss-profile.md`.
- [ ] Pick the vertical with the CEO (`/decide`). Tiebreaker: where you already know people.
- [ ] Register a domain and a business phone number. Open a business bank account. (Under $200.)
- [ ] Record a 3-minute demo: a real call to a sample business handled live by the stack.

**Agents**
- Scout: 300-business prospect list for the vertical and region (name, site, phone, how calls
  are handled today, hook). Verified, not scraped blind.
- Strategist: ideal customer profile, the 3-tier offer finalized, pricing memo.
- Builder: voice stack on Retell/Vapi/Twilio, missed-call text-back, booking link, n8n or Claude
  agent for CRM updates and logging, on the Mac mini. Runbook and kill switch.
- Marketer: one-page site, positioning, the missed-call audit report template.
- Closer: outreach scripts (call, email, LinkedIn), the audit offer, objection sheet.
- CFO: cost per client to deliver, margin per tier, the tax set-aside rule.

## Days 11-30: Sell. Agents prepare everything else.

**Owner, daily**
- [ ] 20 personalized touches a day (call, email, LinkedIn). Lead with the free missed-call audit.
- [ ] Take every discovery call. Offer the first 3 clients a 30-day "pay only if it books" pilot or
      50% off month one. Goal: 1 signed client by day 30.

**Agents, daily**
- Scout: run the audits (5 calls per prospect at different hours), produce the one-page report.
- Closer: personalize each day's 20 touches, log the pipeline, draft follow-ups.
- Marketer: 3-5 LinkedIn posts a week documenting the build. Weekly email draft.
- Builder: onboarding checklist, call scripts per vertical, QA rubric, monthly report template.
- Chief of Staff: daily brief, pipeline in `state.md`, chase Owner to-dos.

## Days 31-60: Deliver flawlessly, productize delivery, ask for referrals

**Owner**
- [ ] Onboard client #1 with a written success metric: calls answered %, leads texted back within
      60s, appointments booked. This is what prevents "the automation became invisible" churn.
- [ ] Keep 20 touches a day. Ask every live client for 2 introductions.
- [ ] 15-minute QA on every go-live. Handle escalations.

**Agents**
- Builder: the pipeline as a system. Intake form, agent configures, human QA, go-live, daily
  transcript monitoring with escalation flags, weekly automated client email with numbers.
- Marketer: before/after case posts from every client.
- CFO: first revenue in the books. Tax set-aside account funded. Cash view weekly.
- Decide at 3 clients: add a white-label CRM/booking layer (about $497/mo platform, resold at
  $297/mo per client) only if 3 or more clients need it.

## Days 61-90: Raise prices, lock retention, start the software ladder

**Owner**
- [ ] New clients on Core or Pro. Founding clients moved to annual plans with a discount.
- [ ] Publish the first real case study with numbers.
- [ ] Recruit 2-3 clients as design partners for the software.

**Agents**
- Strategist and Builder: the software spec. The dashboard, settings and reports every client
  asks for are the product.
- CFO: the day-90 finance review. Revenue, margin, savings rate, next wealth OS milestone.
- Chief of Staff: day-90 review against kill criteria.

## Weekly rhythm

Mon `/brief` and dispatch. Tue-Thu outbound and delivery. Fri pipeline and numbers. Sat
`/upgrade`. Sun `/weekly`. Details in `company/playbooks/weekly-cadence.md`.

## Capital at risk

$300-1,200 total over 90 days: voice minutes, phone numbers, one platform seat, domain. Trading
agents paused, or capped at $500 live with full public logging as content.

## Kill criteria at day 60

Fewer than 1 paying client after 60 days of at least 15 daily touches: switch vertical, not tools.
