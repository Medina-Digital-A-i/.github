# Operation 12 Months — The Honest Plan for Millions, Fast

_Drafted 2026-10-21 by the CEO from four round-3 reports in `company/research/fast/` (about 250
sources). Status: candidate. The Owner decides._

## The truth first

Across deal-fee businesses, international plays, large contracts and product launches, the
researchers could not find one verified case of a person starting with no capital, no license and
no relationships who grossed $1M inside 12 months. Every documented fast winner had at least one
of: their own cash, a license, an existing network, or a distribution channel built before launch.

| Path | Best 12-month P($1M gross) | Median year one | Fee or unit size |
|------|----------------------------|-----------------|------------------|
| Government contracts via teaming/JV with an established small business | 10-15% (15-20% with a veteran, woman-owned or HUBZone status) | $100-300k | awards $0.5-3M, margin 10-20% |
| NYC/NYS no-bid purchases for certified MWBE/SDVOB firms, up to $1.5M | 8-12% if eligible, 0% if not | $325k average award | |
| Land + commercial/multifamily wholesaling | 3% | $60-150k | $8-25k land; $50-250k commercial |
| Transferable clean-energy tax credits | 3% | $0-150k (binary) | $75-300k per deal |
| Lower-middle-market M&A finder | 2% | $0-200k | $150-700k per close |
| LatAm talent + AI managed services | 8-12% gross by month 24 | $150-300k gross | $3-4.5k per seat per month |
| Investor-migration (CBI, E-2 concierge) | 3-5% with Spanish | $20-100k | $25-50k per approved file |
| Product portfolio (4-6 launches) | 4% ARR, 6-8% any $1M outcome | $15-25k | subscriptions |
| Residential wholesaling | 1.5% | $40-120k | $13k average |

So the honest odds of $1M in 12 months on most single paths are 2-4%. The exception is government
contracting through a joint venture with an established small business, where the rules let the
new firm borrow the partner's past performance: 10-15%, and 15-25% when stacked with
micro-purchases and a certification. One caution: that is $1M of contract value, not profit.
Services margins are 10-20%, and a $500k staffing contract needs $80-120k of cash float before
the government pays. Stack the contracting route with the deal tracks and the union gets to
roughly 15-25% for $1M gross within 12-18 months, with a median outcome of $150-300k in year one.
Those are the numbers. I will not inflate them.

## What "think harder" actually yields

The research is clear about where the fat tail lives: **fees on large transactions that need no
license and no capital, where AI collapses the finding and the paperwork, and the human supplies
trust.** Two markets fit that exactly:

1. **Distressed commercial and multifamily real estate, and vacant land.** Public records show
   who is in trouble: loan maturities in the next 12 months, code violations, tax liens, absentee
   land owners of 10+ years. The agents can score tens of thousands of parcels and owners in a
   week and run compliant mail and email. A commercial assignment fee is $50-250k. Six to ten of
   those is a million. Residential wholesaling laws mostly do not reach land and commercial.
2. **Transferable clean-energy tax credits.** A $42B market created in 2023. Small developers
   with $1-15M of credits get two or three bids and are often uninsured. Corporate buyers save
   7-9% on federal taxes. Brokers earn 0.5-3%. The agents can screen every public company's
   filings for tax liability and every energy database for new projects. A $10M credit at 1.25%
   is $125k. The gate is a securities lawyer's opinion and a broker-dealer partner for contingent
   fees. Two companies founded in 2022-2023 did $1M+ in fees in their first 18 months. Both had
   venture money and finance backgrounds. We would have AI and hustle.

Everything else on the list is either a job with a big gross number (staffing), capital-bound
(import, liens, notes), licensed (insurance, yachts, EB-5 commissions), or a lottery ticket
(viral apps).

3. **Government contracts, by teaming.** About 10,000 companies win their first federal contract
   each year. Of the FY2025 set-aside winners the researcher checked, only 6-7% had no prior
   federal history, and half of those were joint ventures. That is the pattern: a new firm
   partners with an established small business under the joint-venture rules, which credit both
   members' past performance, and bids as one. The agents monitor every portal 24/7, draft the
   capability statements and proposals, and build the teaming target list. The Owner meets the
   partner and the contracting officers. Certifications that can be had in under 90 days:
   service-disabled veteran (12 days), HUBZone (60), woman-owned (90). The 8(a) program is frozen
   and not a 12-month path. If the Owner is in New York and eligible for the state or city
   minority- or women-owned business certification, agencies can award up to $1.5M with no bid.

## The plan: one engine, three big-check tracks, one lottery ticket

**Track 0, the cash engine (day 1).** Get-Paid: AI-run receivables recovery for QuickBooks
businesses, performance-priced. It funds the marketing budget the big-check tracks need and it
runs 90% on agents, which keeps the Owner's hours free for deal calls. Target $10-20k/mo by
month 4. (Grant & Bid Studio can run inbound alongside at near-zero Owner time.)

**Track A, land and commercial wholesaling (day 1).** Form the LLC. Pick three counties in
disclosure-only states (Texas, Florida, Georgia, North Carolina, Tennessee, Arizona are
candidates; avoid Pennsylvania and South Carolina; verify each). Agents score 20,000 parcels,
skip-trace 6,000, mail 5,000 letters in week two, build a 300-500 cash-buyer list from deed
records, and in parallel screen 1,500 multifamily owners with loan maturities, liens and
violations. The Owner takes 30-60 seller calls in weeks 3-6. First land assignment ($10-25k) by
day 60; first commercial letter of intent by day 90; first commercial fee ($50k+) by month 6.
Marketing: $4-8k/mo early, rising with fees. Realistic year one: $150-400k. Upside with two
commercial fees: $600k-1M.

**Track B, tax-credit intermediation (day 1, slower cycle).** Week one: securities counsel
opinion on fixed-fee advisory versus contingent fees with a broker-dealer partner. Agents build
the buyer list from public filings (companies with $5-150M of federal tax expense and no prior
credit purchases, about 800 names) and the seller list from energy and permit databases
(developers with 1-5 projects placed in service 2025-2026). The Owner's job is 2-3 signed buyer
mandates by day 60; that is the scarce side. First close month 4-6. One $10M credit at 1.25% is
$125k. Realistic year one: 0-3 closings, $50-350k. Upside with one $40M credit: $500k-1M.

**Track B2, government contracting by teaming (day 1).** Register in SAM.gov and the New York
state and city vendor portals in week one. Agents build a list of 50 established small
businesses in staffing, facilities, admin support, language services and IT with recent
set-aside wins in the Owner's region, and draft the teaming pitch: we bring AI-run proposal
capacity and 24/7 opportunity monitoring, they bring past performance. The Owner meets ten and
signs one joint-venture or subcontract agreement by day 60. In parallel, agents bid every
micro-purchase and simplified-acquisition opportunity under $350k the firm can perform, to build
the first past-performance record. If the Owner qualifies for a certification, file it in
week one. First award target: day 90-120. Line of credit or invoice factoring arranged before
the first award so cash float does not kill the contract.

**Track C, the lottery ticket (Lab).** Two product launches with $5k of creator seeding, run by
the Lab agent with the kill rules from the product report: dead at under $1k/mo by day 60;
double down at $5k/mo growing 30% a month. Expected value is small; the tail is where $1M ARR
and strategic exits live, and it costs the Owner almost no hours.

## What this costs the Owner

- **Hours.** 50-60 a week for the first six months. Deal calls cannot be delegated to agents.
- **Cold calls are back on the table.** Not cold calling strangers from a list; the agents do the
  first touch by mail and email. But every seller who responds and every buyer who matters gets
  a human call, and that is the Owner. There is no 12-month-millions path without it.
- **Cash.** $0-10k gets Track A's first mailing and Track B's counsel started. From month three,
  the cash engine and the first fees fund $5-15k/mo of mail and data. If Track 0 stalls, Track A
  slows with it.
- **Legal lines that are never crossed.** No percentage of capital raised for a syndicator. No
  contingent fees on federal contracts. No brokering equity sales in New York, California,
  Florida or Texas without checking the exemption. No contingent tax-credit fee without counsel.
  No wholesaling in Pennsylvania or South Carolina. No immigration or tax advice.

## Kill criteria and reviews

- Day 60: fewer than one land contract per 40 responses, or under 1% mail response on 10,000
  pieces, switch counties. No buyer mandate on Track B by day 60, drop it to fixed-fee or park it.
- Day 90: fewer than three Get-Paid clients, switch the cash engine to Grant & Bid Studio.
- Day 180: no commercial fee and no tax-credit close, reprice the plan. `/decide` on whether to
  continue Track B.
- Every Sunday: `/weekly` with the pipeline for all three tracks.

## Questions that swing the odds, for the Owner

- Are you a veteran, and if so service-disabled? (12-day certification; sole-source up to $5M.)
- Would the business qualify as woman-owned, or minority-owned under New York's MWBE program?
  (No-bid awards up to $1.5M from city and state agencies.)
- Is your address in a HUBZone? (Check at the SBA map; 60-day certification.)
- Do you speak Spanish?
- Do you know one active commercial real estate buyer, one corporate tax director, one regional
  CPA firm, or one small business that already holds government contracts?

## What would change the odds

- The Owner already knows a vertical or a region from the inside: run Track A there first.
- Spanish: investor-migration intermediation and Latin American nearshoring move up the list
  (roughly 2x the odds, per the international report).
- A single anchor relationship (one active commercial buyer, one corporate tax director, one
  regional CPA firm) is worth more than $20k of marketing. Hunt for it first.
