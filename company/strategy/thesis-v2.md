# Thesis v2 — The AI Back Office (candidate, awaiting Owner decision)

_Drafted 2026-10-21 after the round-2 research. Replaces v1 ("Never Miss a Lead") if the Owner
approves. Evidence: `company/strategy/idea-bank.md` and `company/research/deep/`._

## One paragraph

Medina Digital AI is the AI-run back office for small organizations that have money owed to them,
money available to them, or money denied to them, and nobody to go get it. We start with
**Get-Paid** (collect what QuickBooks businesses are owed, priced on results) and **Grant & Bid
Studio** (win funding for small nonprofits and towns, found inbound). Both are found through the
same door: bookkeepers and CPAs who refer their clients. Profit funds the purchase of a retiring
bookkeeper's practice in year two, which becomes the asset. A small research lane, the Lab, runs
experiments and R&D grants so the Owner builds and discovers, not just approves.

## Why this beats v1

| | v1: Never Miss a Lead | v2: AI Back Office |
|---|---|---|
| How customers are found | 20 cold touches a day to plumbers | Inbound (Upwork, free audits) plus bookkeeper referrals |
| Pricing fight | $497 vs $49-299 apps | Performance fee vs 25-50% collection agencies; $1,250 vs $2-6k grant writers |
| Owner's role | Salesperson | Reviewer, relationship holder, builder |
| AI share of the work | ~80% | ~85-90% |
| End asset | Maybe software | A bought bookkeeping practice plus the software we build around it |
| Evidence quality | 4/5 | 4-5/5 |

## The ladder

1. **Months 0-3, cash:** Get-Paid live with the first five QuickBooks clients, performance-priced.
   Grant & Bid Studio taking inbound work from Upwork and a free readiness score. Target: $5k/mo.
2. **Months 3-9, scale:** bookkeeper referral program (they get a cut). CFO-in-a-Box monthly
   finance pulse as the upsell. Target: $10-15k/mo.
3. **Months 9-18, asset:** bank 50% of profit into an acquisition fund. Buy a $150-300k
   bookkeeping or tax practice from a retiring owner with seller financing. Run its back office
   with agents.
4. **Years 2-4, roll-up and product:** add Denial Desk for the medical practices in the book. Add
   two more practices. Convert the shared workflows into a self-serve tool, built the way the
   documented winners did it: one narrow job, a 60-second demo, charge from day one,
   subscriptions, go upmarket before a platform vendor copies it.
5. **Years 5-7, exit or hold:** a bookkeeping group with AI delivery and a software layer is a
   $1M+ asset on the evidence, on top of the cash saved along the way.

## The Lab

A capped research lane (one day a week of agent time, the Owner's choice of hours) run by the
Lab agent. First jobs: three SBIR topics that fit, experiments that improve our own products, and
one small software acquisition as a training exercise. Science is a loop: question, test, learn.

## The Owner's job

Review what the agents produce, hold the bookkeeper relationships, take warm calls, approve money,
and spend the rest of the time in the Lab. No cold calling.

## Kill criteria

Fewer than three paying Get-Paid clients after 60 days of live referral and inbound effort: the
channel is wrong, switch to Grant & Bid Studio as the core. Fewer than $5k/mo by month 4: run
`/decide` on Denial Desk or Home-Turf Tool.
